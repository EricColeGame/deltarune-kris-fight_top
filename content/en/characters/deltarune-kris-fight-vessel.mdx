## The Origins: The Discarded Goner Vessel and Kris's Autonomy

In the introduction of Chapter 1, players step into the "GONER MAKER" sequence, meticulously assembling an avatar from various physical parts and answering personality inquiries. Right after naming the creation and its creator, the interface shatters with a cold declaration: *"No one can choose who they are in this world."* The vessel is discarded, and control shifts abruptly to Kris lying in bed in Hometown.

Despite the narrator claiming the body was thrown away, data mining reveals that the game writes every single choice—head type, torso, legs, favorite food, blood type, and assigned name—directly into the save file variables. This persistent data demonstrates that the creation was never truly deleted. 

Player experience and community data miners have traced these parameters across subsequent chapters, noting that Toby Fox rarely introduces stateful variables without paying them off later. Kris, meanwhile, repeatedly demonstrates an uncanny resistance to the player's red SOUL, ripping it out of their chest during nocturnal sequences to pursue their own mysterious agenda.

## The Narrative Impact on Player Choice and Agency

The thematic core of *Deltarune* revolves around whether choices matter. In *Undertale*, player choices completely altered the world state, leading to radically different endings ranging from True Pacifist to Genocide. *Deltarune* subverts this by claiming that only one ending exists, regardless of the paths taken.

However, the narrative stakes of the **Deltarune Kris fight vessel** debate call this absolute claim into question. By juxtaposing Kris—a teenager with an established history, friendships, and traumas—against a nameless vessel designed by the player, Toby Fox highlights the ethical dilemma of piloting someone else’s body against their will.

* Kris hesitates when speaking certain lines of dialogue forced by the player.
* Susie notices when Kris looks uncomfortable or distressed after intense encounters.
* The birdcage in Kris’s bedroom displays dents and stains from previous SOUL removals.
* The vessel remains dormant, devoid of a past, waiting to be filled with the player's direct will.

If the story culminates in a direct duel between these two entities, it forces players to confront their own complicity. Are we heroes guiding lightners to seal fountains, or are we an alien entity hijacking a child’s life when our real creation sits abandoned in the digital dark?
