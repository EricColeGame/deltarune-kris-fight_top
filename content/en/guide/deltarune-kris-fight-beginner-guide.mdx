## Understanding Kris’s Core Combat Role and Turn Flow

In battle, Kris functions as the party's primary tactician and decision-maker. While Susie specializes in physical brawn and Ralsei handles restorative magic, Kris holds the unique ability to interact directly with enemies through the ACT command. Even when you instruct Susie or Ralsei to act, their unique actions are orchestrated through Kris’s tactical turn.

Every battle in the game follows a strict, two-phase turn structure:

1. **Party Command Phase:** You queue up actions for Kris, Susie, and Ralsei (or any temporary ally joining your party). Commands include FIGHT, ACT, ITEM, SPARE, and DEFEND.
2. **Enemy Bullet Board Phase:** Once all party actions conclude, the battlefield transitions into an interactive bullet-hell dodge phase. Here, you control the red SOUL directly to weave between enemy projectiles.

Because Kris is typically the first character to act in the turn order, their decisions dictate the tempo of the entire encounter. Choosing to defend with Kris can generate immediate resources, while choosing an ACT command sets the stage for pacifying foes before they can inflict heavy damage on your squad.

| Battle Command | Primary Function | TP Impact | Best Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **FIGHT** | Deals physical damage via a timed strike bar | None (Generates none) | Violent routes or weakening specific non-recruit enemies |
| **ACT** | Triggers personality-based events to raise Mercy | Consumes 0% TP (Standard) | Pacifying regular enemies and progressing boss gimmicks |
| **ITEM** | Uses consumable food, restoratives, or tools | None | Emergency healing or revitalizing downed allies |
| **SPARE** | Removes 100% Mercy enemies from combat | None | Ending encounters peacefully without gaining EXP/violence |
| **DEFEND** | Reduces incoming damage and guards the user | Generates +16% TP | Preparing for heavy attacks or stocking TP for party spells |

Choosing when to defend with Kris is just as vital as choosing when to strike. Community reports highlight that players who utilize the DEFEND command frequently experience significantly fewer party wipes during early boss fights.

## The Art of ACTing and Sparing: Peaceful Encounter Strategies

One core lesson of this **deltarune kris fight beginner guide** is that combat resolution does not always require draining an enemy's hit points. In fact, sparing enemies rewards you with recruits for your Castle Town and reveals unique dialogue. Kris is the linchpin of the Mercy system because standard enemies cannot be spared until their Mercy bar reaches 100%, which is achieved almost entirely through Kris’s ACT menu.

Kris can perform standard individual ACTs or coordinate group actions known as S-ACT (Susie ACT) and R-ACT (Ralsei ACT). These collaborative maneuvers allow your companions to perform custom interactions without sacrificing their ability to learn standard enemy behaviors.

```
Individual ACT (Kris Solo) ──> Increases Mercy slightly; leaves companions free to Defend or Heal.
Dual / Team ACT (Kris + Ally) ──> Substantially raises Mercy; consumes companion's turn.
```

To maximize your tactical efficiency, review how different ACT types influence enemy psychological states during combat.

| Action Type | Initiating Characters | Turn Economy Cost | Strategic Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Solo ACT** | Kris only | 1 Party Member Turn | Frees Susie and Ralsei to DEFEND for TP or cast support spells |
| **S-ACT** | Kris directs Susie | 2 Party Member Turns | High Mercy gain against brute-force or rebellious enemy types |
| **R-ACT** | Kris directs Ralsei | 2 Party Member Turns | Calms agitated enemies; often synergizes with Ralsei's Pacify spell |
| **X-ACT** | Kris directs entire team | 3 Party Member Turns | Maximum Mercy yield; instantly resolves many multi-enemy turns |

According to player experience across early chapters, utilizing R-ACT alongside Ralsei’s *Pacify* spell is the fastest way to conclude multi-target encounters. Once an enemy becomes "Tired" (indicated by drowsy animations and blue text), Ralsei can cast Pacify for 16% TP to instantly remove them from combat. Following the strategies outlined in this **deltarune kris fight beginner guide** will help you streamline these turns without taking unnecessary hits.

## Tension Points (TP) and Evasion: Surviving the Bullet Board

Outside of basic commands, your combat effectiveness relies heavily on Tension Points (TP). The TP gauge, displayed along the left side of the screen, starts at 0% in each battle and caps at 100%. Advanced spells—such as Susie's offensive skills or Ralsei's *Heal Prayer*—rely entirely on having sufficient TP. 

Kris does not have an extensive personal magic list, but Kris plays a massive role in generating TP for the squad. There are two primary ways to generate TP during combat:

1. **Defending:** Selecting DEFEND on Kris or any ally grants an immediate +16% TP boost per defending character at the start of the turn.
2. **Grazing Projectiles:** During the enemy's attack phase, steering Kris’s red SOUL very close to incoming white bullets without touching them "grazes" the hazard. Each graze creates a distinct sound cue, outlines your SOUL in white, and immediately refills a slice of the TP meter while simultaneously reducing the turn timer.

| Evasion / Defense Technique | TP Generation Rate | Risk Level | Primary Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Defend** | +16% TP per member | Zero | Halves incoming damage taken by the defending character |
| **Peripheral Grazing** | +1% to +3% per bullet | Low | Safely builds meter against slow, predictable projectile rings |
| **Aggressive Close Grazing** | +5% to +10% per wave | High | Quickly caps TP gauge; drastically cuts duration of the enemy turn |
| **Corner Camping** | Minimal (0%) | Moderate | Avoids complex bullet geometry but yields zero tactical resources |

Mastering the SOUL's movement speed is crucial. If you hold the cancel button (such as Shift on PC or the designated face button on consoles), your SOUL slows down, allowing for precise micro-movements between tight bullet spreads. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Kris have personal magic spells in combat?
Unlike Susie and Ralsei, Kris does not feature a dedicated MAGIC menu under standard conditions. Instead, Kris’s tactical utility stems from the ACT menu, which allows Kris to alter enemy stats, progress encounter-specific puzzles, and command party members to execute specialized support tasks.

### Why is this deltarune kris fight beginner guide focused on ACTing instead of attacking?
While attacking with the FIGHT command is a valid way to defeat opponents, ACTing allows you to recruit enemies, unlock exclusive story sequences, and avoid lethal violence. Sparing foes through ACTs also ensures your party stays true to the game's core theme of mercy, which is heavily emphasized throughout modern RPG design.

### What is the most common mistake beginners make during the bullet board phase?
The most frequent mistake is over-moving. Beginners often panic and dart across the entire bullet box, running directly into trailing hazards. Keeping movements small, utilizing the slow-movement focus button, and staying centered makes dodging far more consistent.

### Can the party continue fighting if Kris gets knocked out?
Yes. If Kris reaches 0 HP, Susie and Ralsei can continue fighting and can use consumable healing items to revive Kris. However, while Kris is unconscious, your party loses access to Kris's specialized ACT commands, severely limiting your ability to spare foes until Kris is brought back into the fight.
