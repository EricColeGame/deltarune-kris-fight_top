## Turn Economy and Tension Point (TP) Management

Tension Points (TP) power the strongest spells and abilities in your arsenal, including Ralsei’s Heal Prayer, Susie’s Rude Buster, and Kris’s devastating X-Slash. A core pillar of any refined deltarune kris fight strategy is generating and spending TP with zero wasted overhead.

You generate TP through two primary methods: Defending during the decision phase and Grazing enemy projectiles during the evasion phase. Defending immediately grants 16% TP per defending party member while reducing incoming damage for that turn by roughly 50% to 66%. Grazing requires you to position the Red Soul dangerously close to enemy projectiles without touching their active hitboxes.

### Jevil (Chapter 1 Secret Boss)

Jevil represents the first true test of mechanical execution in the game. His attacks follow strict mathematical rotations that demand precise rhythmic movement.

When crafting a deltarune kris fight strategy against Jevil, decide early between Hypnosis and Pirouette. Hypnosis costs 32% TP, reduces Jevil's stamina significantly, and slightly reduces Jevil's attack stat for the following turn. Pirouette costs only 16% TP but triggers rotating, semi-random effects based strictly on the current turn counter.

### Spamton NEO (Chapter 2 Secret Boss)

Facing Spamton NEO transforms your combat interface by converting Kris's Red Soul into the Yellow Soul, capable of firing offensive charge shots. A specialized deltarune kris fight strategy for this encounter emphasizes firing discipline and target prioritization.

1.  **Use Charged Shots for Obstacle Clearing:** Holding the fire button unleashes a piercing blast that neutralizes incoming pipis bombs and floating head projectiles.
2.  **Snap and SnapPress ACTs:** When attempting a pacifist clear, Kris must alternate between Snap and SnapPress. SnapPress requires party coordination to sever multiple wires simultaneously, demanding 16% TP.
3.  **Heart Attack Phase Mitigation:** When Spamton exposes his heart, prioritize rapid tap-firing over charged shots to maximize hit count before the heart retreats.
4.  **Managing the Phone Hands Attack:** Keep the Soul positioned along the far left edge of the arena, moving in small vertical sweeps rather than erratic circular motions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most reliable deltarune kris fight strategy for secret bosses?
The most dependable deltarune kris fight strategy against secret bosses involves using Kris as a dedicated ACT manager while assigning Susie and Ralsei to Defend for TP generation. This setup ensures a constant supply of Tension Points for high-tier recovery magic like Heal Prayer or turn-skipping ACTs like Hypnosis, while minimizing incoming damage to your supporting party members.

### Should Kris always focus on ACTing instead of attacking?
In standard pacifist playthroughs, Kris should almost exclusively select ACT commands or Defend to generate TP. In violent or aggressive routes, Kris's role shifts toward heavy physical offense using weapons like the Mecha Saber or Auto Axe, capitalizing on high-damage skills like X-Slash whenever TP reaches 25%.

### How does grazing impact Kris's survival during tough battles?
Grazing incoming projectiles increases your Tension Bar without requiring you to expend turns on the Defend command. Additionally, grazing slightly reduces the total turn duration of certain boss attack patterns, allowing you to return to the decision phase faster while accumulating the resources needed for game-winning spells.

### What equipment provides the best defense for Kris in Chapter 2?
For optimal survivability, pair the Mecha Saber with defensive armor like the Dealmaker and the Royal Pin or Chain Mail. This combination grants top-tier physical Defense, substantial elemental resistance, and generous TP generation bonuses that enhance your entire party's combat efficiency.
