### Can Kris act independently during regular combat turns?

### What happens if Kris falls to zero HP in battle?
When Kris is downed, they enter a unconscious state until revived by an item or healing spell. While Kris is down, standard ACT commands become unavailable unless party ACTs have been assigned.
