## Attack Patterns, Dodging Strategies, and Bullet Hell Breakdown

Spamton NEO utilizes a frantic arsenal of projectile attacks designed to overwhelm traditional dodging tactics. When facing these patterns solo, your Yellow SOUL's charged shots ("Big Shots") serve as both defensive clearing tools and offensive harassers.

       └── Heart on a Chain ───► Alternate between micro-dodging and direct heart bursts

The heart on a chain attack requires deliberate pacing. Rather than holding down continuous fire, time your charge shots to break the outer shielding before shifting into vertical micro-adjustments. 

| Attack Wave Name | Danger Level | Primary Threat | Evasion & Counter Strategy |

Surviving the bullet patterns during the **deltarune kris fight weird route** battle hinges on precise grazing and charged shots. Grazing bullets not only builds the TP needed to sustain yourself, but it also shortens turn durations, reducing your overall exposure to lethal hitboxes.

## Turn-by-Turn Action Economy: ACTing vs. X-Slash and FriedPipis

Without companions, Kris’s turn-by-turn action economy is strictly divided between three tactical actions: spending TP on **X-Slash**, using the unique weird route command **FriedPipis**, or burning an item slot to heal.

Managing tension points is the core engine of this battle. FriedPipis costs 32% TP and restores roughly 120 HP, providing a repeatable sustain engine without consuming limited inventory items. However, relying solely on healing drags the fight out, increasing the probability of mechanical errors during Spamton’s escalating bullet waves.

| Action Command | TP Cost | Turn Impact | Optimal Usage Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **X-Slash** | 25% TP | Deals massive physical damage | Whenever HP is above 75% and TP is readily available |
| **FriedPipis** | 32% TP | Restores ~120 HP | When HP drops below 50% to save valuable inventory items |
| **Standard Attack** | 0% TP | Moderate damage; generates TP | Building TP during predictable, low-threat attack waves |
| **Inventory Consumable** | 0% TP | Direct heal (70–80 HP) | Emergency stabilization when TP is depleted below 32% |
| **Defend** | 0% TP | +16% TP gain, reduces damage taken | Pre-empting unavoidable heavy-damage phases |

Balancing spell points during the **deltarune kris fight weird route** encounter requires deliberate action economy choices. A proven rotation among experienced players is: **Attack -> Graze to build TP -> X-Slash -> Defend/Heal**. Never let your TP sit capped at 100%; immediately convert excess tension into offensive burst via X-Slash to shorten the encounter.

The battle progresses through distinct damage thresholds, each ramping up the projectile density and introducing chaotic variations of previous attacks. Keeping track of Spamton NEO's remaining health allows you to anticipate desperate phase transitions.

### Phase 1: Opening Gambit (100% to 75% HP)
The boss begins with standard bullet patterns. Use this phase to establish your shot rhythm. Charge your Big Shot before the attack box even spawns so you can immediately blast incoming obstacles. Do not waste inventory consumables here; rely exclusively on standard attacks and X-Slash.

### Phase 2: Pipis Overload (75% to 40% HP)
Spamton increases the frequency of Pipis projectiles. If an egg reaches the left boundary, it explodes into a dense scatter of shrapnel that is nearly impossible to dodge cleanly. Keep your SOUL centered along the vertical axis and tap the fire button rapidly rather than holding for charge shots.

### Phase 3: The Enraged Assault (40% to 10% HP)
The attacks become combined. You will frequently face moving mouth trains while phone hands sweep across the bottom of the arena. Save your high-tier healing items (like the SpinCake) for this stretch. Use FriedPipis whenever your health dips below 60 to prevent being one-shot by stray hits.

| **Phase 2** | 74% – 40% | High-velocity Pipis spawns | Rapid-fire uncharged shots; prioritize clearing screen space |
| **Phase 3** | 39% – 10% | Combined pattern onslaught | Use FriedPipis; preserve top-tier inventory healing |
| **Phase 4** | < 10% | Defense buff & scripted calls | Select scripted Call ACT commands; focus purely on evasion |

Navigating the closing turns of the **deltarune kris fight weird route** climax tests your ability to adapt under pressure. Once Spamton's defense surges near the end of the fight, physical attacks become largely ineffective. You must survive the final onslaught while selecting the scripted "Call" options. When calling for Susie and Ralsei yields no response, making the final call to Noelle triggers the dramatic resolution of the route.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Deltarune Kris Fight

### Why is the deltarune kris fight weird route so much harder than the standard route?
The weird route iteration forces a 1v1 solo engagement against Spamton NEO. In the standard route, damage is distributed across Kris, Susie, and Ralsei, and players have access to Ralsei's reliable healing spells. Solo Kris must handle attacking, healing, and dodging simultaneously without any backup.

### Can you spare Spamton NEO in the weird route?
No. Unlike the standard path where you can sever Spamton's wires to spare him peacefully, the weird route requires you to deplete his health bar or progress the scripted combat commands to conclusion. Pacifist Mercy mechanics are disabled for this encounter.

### What does the FriedPipis ACT command do?
FriedPipis is an exclusive ACT command available to Kris during this solo duel. It costs 32% TP and restores approximately 120 HP. It functions as your primary regenerative tool, allowing you to conserve pocket inventory items for emergency situations.

### What happens if I run out of healing items during the battle?
If your inventory runs dry, you must rely entirely on grazing incoming projectiles to generate the 32% TP needed for FriedPipis, or use the Defend command (+16% TP per turn) to build tension quickly while halving incoming damage.
