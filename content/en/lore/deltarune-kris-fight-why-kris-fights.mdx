## Tactical Tips for Mastering Combat as Kris

Navigating battles efficiently requires understanding both the turn economy and the graze mechanics that define modern bullet-hell RPGs. Kris's stats make them the most versatile unit on the field, capable of salvaging near-wipe situations when managed properly.

### 1. Optimize the Graze Perimeter
Grazing enemy bullets charges the Tension Point (TP) meter rapidly. Because Kris operates with consistent agility, position Kris's SOUL near the edge of projectile streams during early bullet cycles. This generates the necessary TP to cast Ralsei's pacifying spells or Susie's high-damage skills on turn one or two.

### 2. Prioritize Defensive Synergy
When facing aggressive bosses like Jevil or Spamton NEO, dedicate Kris to using the DEFEND action on turns where incoming damage patterns are erratic. Defending not only lowers incoming damage by approximately 50%, but it also yields 16% TP instantly, allowing backline spellcasters to heal or use ACT synergies without sacrificing momentum.

### 3. Exploit S-Action and Dual Acts
In Chapter 2 and beyond, Kris gains the ability to command party members to act independently using S-Actions. By delegating Susie or Ralsei to handle specific enemy interactions, Kris remains free to use healing items, defend, or initiate critical recruit prerequisites.
