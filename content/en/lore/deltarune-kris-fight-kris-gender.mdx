### Tension Point Management and the Grazing Advantage

Tension Points represent the lifeblood of difficult encounters. Kris's soul—controlled directly by the player—must actively graze enemy bullet patterns to charge the TP gauge. Grazing reduces the distance between the glowing heart hitbox and incoming projectiles, rewarding high-risk maneuvering with rapid resource regeneration.

Defending with Kris yields 16% TP instantly while reducing damage taken on the subsequent turn. When facing complex bullet storms, placing Kris in a defensive stance while Susie or Ralsei prepares a game-changing spell is often the safest operational protocol.
