## Grazing, Tension Points (TP), and Tactical Action

Grazing represents the bridge between defensive survival and offensive capability. TP fuels your party's most potent abilities, including Ralsei's Heal Prayer, Susie's Red Buster, and specialized Kris ACTs like Courage or Pirouette. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the primary difference in the deltarune kris fight soul mechanic compared to Undertale?
While *Undertale* restricted combat strictly to solo encounters where the SOUL represented the protagonist's direct life force, the deltarune kris fight soul framework integrates party-based turn mechanics. You control the SOUL to protect the collective party, while grazing generates Tension Points (TP) that fuel team-wide spells and unique ACT commands.

### Can Kris survive in combat if the SOUL runs out of HP?
No. If the SOUL's hit points reach zero during the defensive board phase, the party suffers a collective defeat, triggering the "THE WORLD IS COVERED IN DARKNESS" game over screen. Kris's individual defense stat mitigates how much damage the SOUL takes when struck by enemy bullets targeting Kris.

### How does grazing impact overall battle duration?
Grazing dramatically shortens battles. Near-misses with the SOUL generate TP quickly, enabling frequent casting of offensive spells like Susie's Red Buster or encounter-ending pacification spells like Ralsei's Pacify. This reduces the total number of defensive dodging turns required to win.

### Why does Kris remove their SOUL during cutscenes?
Narratively, the red SOUL represents player agency and control over the game world. Kris removes the SOUL to regain physical autonomy, allowing them to perform deliberate, unguided actions—such as plugging in the television, creating fountains, or moving independently—outside the player's direct oversight.
