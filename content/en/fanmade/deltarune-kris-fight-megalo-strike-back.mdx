## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Megalo Strike Back battle officially canon to Deltarune?
No, the encounter is entirely fanmade. While "Megalo Strike Back" was composed by Toby Fox for an unrelated 2012 EarthBound tribute album, the battle itself was conceptualized and programmed by passionate members of the *Undertale* and *Deltarune* community.

### Why is Megalo Strike Back used instead of Megalovania?
"Megalovania" is inextricably tied to the Sans genocide battle in *Undertale*. Fan creators chose "Megalo Strike Back" for Kris because of its historical community association with rogue human protagonists and its relentless, aggressive rhythm, which matches Kris's swordplay.
