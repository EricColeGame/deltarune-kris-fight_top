4. **Bait the Thrust Attack:** By hovering just outside Kris's normal swing radius, you can bait a lunging thrust. Step sideways just as Kris lunges forward, then punish their recovery animation with two to three heavy hits.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Deltarune Kris Fight Roblox

### Why am I taking damage even when I dodge Kris's sword slashes?
This is almost always due to Roblox network latency or lingering attack hitboxes. If you dodge too late, your client-side character might appear clear, but the server calculates that you were within the blade's path. To avoid this, initiate your dash based on the boss's startup animation rather than reacting after the sword is already swinging.

### Can you spare Kris in a deltarune kris fight roblox game, or must you fight?
Depending on the specific fan game, many developers include both FIGHT and MERCY paths. Sparing Kris usually requires using specialized ACT commands—such as reasoning, deflecting attacks without retaliating, or surviving a specific number of turns—until Kris's MERCY bar fills to 100%.

### What is the best camera setting for surviving bullet-hell phases?
Player experience strongly recommends using a top-down or high-angle third-person camera with Shift-Lock enabled. This gives you maximum visibility over incoming ground hazards and allows for instantaneous micro-adjustments that standard over-the-shoulder perspectives obscure.

### How can I practice dodging Kris's hardest attack patterns?
Many popular battle places include a "Practice Mode" or "Attack Selector" in the main lobby. Utilizing these tools allows you to practice individual bullet patterns—such as the Desperation Flurry—without having to replay the earlier phases of the encounter repeatedly.
